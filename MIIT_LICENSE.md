## Service description

LiveGuard is designed to facilitate emergency reporting and response coordination across civilians and authorized emergency agencies. The platform enables users to submit incident alerts, share location data, and receive real-time acknowledgement and response updates. The backend evaluates incident risk, assigns appropriate agencies, and dispatches alerts through push notifications, SMS, and email.
